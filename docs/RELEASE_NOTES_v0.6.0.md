## ThermaLabel v0.6.0

First public release.

- **Label editor**: text, rectangle, circle, triangle, line and frame elements, with stroke and *Corner round* controls. Drag, resize, nudge, duplicate.
- **Tiling**: *Columns* x *Rows* of labels in one print, with *Gap*, *Margin* and optional *Cut lines*.
- **Procedural frames**: single, double, dashed, dotted, scalloped, corner brackets, ticket, stamp, thick + thin.
- **11 embedded fonts**, works offline.
- **Food label preset** and *Quick fill* with one-tap "use by" days; date tokens `{today}`, `{+3}`, `{weekday}`.
- **Exact 1-bit preview** of the dots sent to the printer.
- **Templates** in the browser plus JSON export/import.
- **Vretti TP6-S driver** with live status (battery, paper, cover, overheating) and *Auto* transfer.
- *Printer* selector ready for more models (MUNBYN 130B next).

Run: open `ThermaLabel_v0.6.0.html` in Chrome or Edge, or online at https://kumodot.github.io/ThermaLabel/
